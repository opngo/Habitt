Download View on GitHub

Everything you need

A complete system for building habits, tracking tasks, and reflecting on your progress

GitHub-Style Heatmap

Beautiful year/month/week activity grids with rich tooltips showing completed habits and notes per day.

Tasks & Subtasks

Full task management with priorities, due dates, tags, subtasks, and status tracking. Kanban-style organization.

AI Journaling

Ollama-powered AI reflections, writing prompts, and mood analysis. Export to Obsidian daily notes format.

Homework Tracker

Color-coded assignments by subject. Custom subjects with icons. Due date tracking with overdue alerts.

Focus Timer

Pomodoro timer with presets, round tracking, session history, and XP rewards. Tie sessions to specific habits.

3 Habit Types

Normal (check off), Avoid (track relapses), and Amount (quantity goals with units). Checklists for multi-step habits.

Rich Statistics

Streak leaderboards, day-of-week patterns, monthly trends, habit strength scores, and hourly consistency analysis.

Notes with Tags

Quick notes with colors, icons, tags, and pinning. Search across all notes. Edit inline.

Gamification

XP system, 28+ achievements, levels, confetti celebrations, and streak milestones to keep you motivated.

Built with modern tools

React 18 Reshaped UI Lucide Icons Tauri 2 Rust SQLite Zustand Ollama AI Obsidian Export
